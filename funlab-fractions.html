Why it matters

Higher-class maths stands on fractions

Algebra, ratios, percentages, probability, and the formulas in physics and chemistry all use fractions. Without this base, children struggle to follow what is taught in Classes 9–12. The best time to fix it is now, before Class 9.

Just like cricket

Scoring in maths is like hitting 4s and 6s in cricket

In cricket, 4s and 6s put big runs on the board quickly, while running only singles and doubles is slow and tiring. Maths is the same: with a strong foundation, marks come quickly and easily.

Net practice

Champions are made in the nets

Great cricketers practise every day, and maths works the same way. Regular practice builds maths muscle. In our workshop we practise playfully, with games, so it never feels like a chore.

Hidden gaps

School marks may not show it

Many children don’t even know they are unclear on fractions. Our free Fraction Fitness Check shows whether the foundation is strong. If it shows gaps, your child can join the workshop.

Not tuition

A workshop kids enjoy

  • Learn a little, play a lot with games and hands-on fraction strips
  • Learn together in a small, friendly group
  • Confidence grows with every small win

Master one concept well, and children start wanting to learn the next one too.
